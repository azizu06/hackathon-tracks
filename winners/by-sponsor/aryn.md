## 2025 winners

### [GauchoHome](https://devpost.com/software/homelessgaucho)

[SB Hacks XI 2025](../by-hackathon/sb-hacks-xi-2025.md#project-homelessgaucho) · **Best use of Sycamore**

Helps UCSB and SBCC students in IV locate housing. Powered by a data ingestion pipeline that runs on Claude and Aryn to aggregate housing information.

**How it used Aryn:** The team don't generate anything new with Claude, and all of the actual information comes from Aryn's parsing.

[Code](https://github.com/youwen5/gauchohousing) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NNIs7zczSBQ) · Built with aryn, claude, django, google-maps

### [NabuAI](https://devpost.com/software/nobuai)

[SB Hacks XI 2025](../by-hackathon/sb-hacks-xi-2025.md#project-nobuai) · **Best use of Sycamore**

In addition, the live scraping of academic databases and the future goals of scraping news articles and more sources will keep all the information up-to-date.

[Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L52sdmP66Yo) · Built with aryn, firebase, flask, gpt

### [Share Tome](https://devpost.com/software/share-tome)

[SB Hacks XI 2025](../by-hackathon/sb-hacks-xi-2025.md#project-share-tome) · **Best use of Sycamore**

Drag and drop, shareable and searchable vector databases.

**How it used Aryn:** ShareTome is built with a Go backend, Next.JS Frontend, Aryn Sycamore for document parsing and uploading, and ElasticSearch as a vector store / search engine.

Built with aryn, elasticsearch, go, next.js
