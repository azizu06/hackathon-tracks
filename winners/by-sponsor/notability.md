### [ATP - Accessible to Prescription](https://devpost.com/software/atp)

[HackGT 13 2026](../by-hackathon/hackgt-13-2026.md#project-atp) · **Trust the Process**

ATP speeds up prescription prior authorizations. AI handles insurer calls, brings in doctors when clinical judgment is needed, and sends the outcome back to their workflow.

**How it used Notability:** The team used Notability as the digital whiteboard to sketch the call flow, map the AI-to-doctor handoff, and plan features before building them.

### [Inkling](https://devpost.com/software/inkling-ud1re3)
