### [CrowdClear](https://devpost.com/software/crowdclear)

### [Hazard Intelligence](https://devpost.com/software/hazard-intelligence)

[MHacks 2026](../by-hackathon/mhacks-2026.md#project-hazard-intelligence) · **Best Use of Presage**

A robotics team collects human skiing and hiking motions to train a humanoid on outdoor terrain.

**How it used Presage:** Using Presage’s SmartSpectra, the webcam reads heart rate and breathing to identify, on average, the more physically demanding parts of the path.

[Code](https://github.com/RyanRana/skibot) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OutFnDuQV00) · Built with presage, cloudflare, core-location, coremotion

### [MedGuard](https://devpost.com/software/medguard-dav58h)

[Hack@Brown 2026](../by-hackathon/hack-brown-2026.md#project-medguard-dav58h) · **Best Use of Presage**

Gene+Drug+Drug interaction app with live computer vision made for patients.

**How it used Presage:** The team integrated the Presage SDK to measure heart rate and breathing rate via the iPhone camera.

[Code](https://github.com/yaznzee/MedGuard) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qSndyrIHC8Y) · Built with swift, xcode

### [ThirdEye](https://devpost.com/software/thirdeye-a74whv)

### [UnderPressure](https://devpost.com/software/underpressure)

[Code](https://github.com/ethernetvan/UnderPressure) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UIsO8ZmOv4Y) · Built with presage, elevenlabs, facemesh, gemini

### [VibeSense](https://devpost.com/software/vibesense)

[nwHacks 2026](../by-hackathon/nwhacks-2026.md#project-vibesense) · **Best Use of Presage**

An accessibility tool converts facial expressions into audio cues for blind users.

**How it used Presage:** Biometric Integration: The system integrates with Presage technology to collect heartbeat and breathing rate data, providing additional physiological context that supports and enhances emotion detection accuracy.

[Code](https://github.com/havido/VibeSense) · [Demo video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tY2O1hSmsxo) · Built with presage, arduino, deepface, flask
